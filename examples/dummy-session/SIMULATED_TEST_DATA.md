These records were generated by portable Lua integration tests.
They are not participant results and must not enter the study dataset.

with artificial timings/counts. Final video-coded measures are blank.
No real Mac, Chrome, Hammerspoon input hooks or OBS recording was used.
Any saved machine paths refer to the temporary test environment; do not use
this folder as an operational session or copy it into records/main.
